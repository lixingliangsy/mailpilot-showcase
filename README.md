# MailPilot

> AI email auto-reply workflows for busy support inboxes.

**Category:** Customer Support

**Live product:** [https://mailpilot.lxsaihub.com](https://mailpilot.lxsaihub.com)

## Highlights

- 5-type email classification
- Sentiment & urgency scoring
- Branded draft templates with variables
- Human escalation rules
- Transparent decision flow
## What it does

MailPilot is a developer-friendly email auto-reply workflow for e-commerce support teams drowning in 200+ customer emails a day. It classifies every message (inquiry / complaint / return / partnership / spam), detects sentiment and urgency, drafts on-brand replies with your variables, and escalates the risky ones to a human — with a transparent decision flow you can audit.
